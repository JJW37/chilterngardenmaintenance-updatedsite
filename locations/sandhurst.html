Local conditions in Sandhurst

Geology and soil

Bagshot Formation sand and Bracklesham Beds. Acidic, free-draining sand.

Soil type: Free-draining sandy loam. Light and easy to work but drought-prone in summer.

Soil pH: Acidic, pH 5.0 to 6.0. Ideal for acid-loving plants.

Rainfall

Annual rainfall: Approximately 700mm per year.

Monthly pattern: Wettest October to January at 70mm. Driest July and August at 45mm.

Temperature

Annual average 10.5C. Summer highs 22C. Winter lows 1C.

Garden types in the area

Family homes, properties near the Academy, modern developments. Gardens tend to be medium-sized.

Local conditions to consider

Acidic free-draining sand suits heathers, conifers and rhododendrons. Drought is the main challenge in summer. Pine trees provide shade but make soil more acidic.
