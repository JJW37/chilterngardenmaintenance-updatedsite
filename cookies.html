Cookie Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026

Cookies are small files and similar technologies that a website can store or access on your device. This page explains what CGM uses and how you can control it.

Strictly necessary preference storage

When you choose Accept or Decline on our cookie banner, the website stores your choice in your browser’s local storage under cgm_cookie_consent (and a legacy cookieConsent value). This is used only to remember your choice and is necessary for the consent mechanism to work.

Analytics

If you choose Accept, the site may load Google Analytics to help us understand how the website is used, such as pages visited and general device/browser information. We do not load the Google Analytics library before that choice. Choosing Decline prevents that analytics library from loading.

Managing your choice

You can change your choice at any time by clearing this site’s browser storage/cookies and revisiting the site, or by using your browser’s privacy controls. Blocking all cookies may affect features that need to remember a setting.

Third-party sites

Links to services such as WhatsApp, Google Maps and social media take you to third-party websites. Their cookies and privacy practices are governed by their own policies.
