01 / The brief

See the decisions, not just the result.

Each numbered view becomes a matched frame: a small moment in the job and a way to move through the property at your own pace.

The system recognises before / during / after labels, including extra “after” views, and builds the story from the supplied folder.

03 / Walk the frames

A guided route through the job.

The walkthrough is an optional layer for people who want to be led. It moves through every numbered view, while the filmstrip keeps the visitor in control.

Pause, step back, step forward or jump to a frame. No autoplay is forced on the visitor.
